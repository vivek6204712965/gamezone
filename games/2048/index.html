About 2048 Deluxe

2048 Deluxe is an elegant, responsive web edition of the viral mathematical slide-and-merge puzzle. Featuring smooth sliding animations, vivid neon tile palettes, undo functionality, and persistent high score tracking, this browser version is engineered for both quick casual sessions and deep strategic mastery on desktop and mobile.

How to Play

Use your keyboard arrow keys, WASD, or swipe gestures to slide all tiles simultaneously across the 4x4 grid in your chosen direction. When two tiles displaying the same number collide during a slide, they combine into a single tile with double the value (e.g., 2+2=4, 4+4=8, 1024+1024=2048). After every valid move, a new tile (valued 2 or 4) spawns randomly in an empty space.

Game Controls

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D — Slide tiles
  • ↶ Undo — Reverse your previous move
  • New Game — Reset and restart board

📱 Mobile & Touch Controls

  • Swipe Gestures — Swipe up, down, left, right on the board
  • ▲ ▼ ◀ ▶ — Tap on-screen directional buttons

Rules & Corner Strategy Tips

  • The Corner Anchor: Choose one corner (usually bottom-right or bottom-left) and build your largest tile there. Avoid sliding away from your chosen corner.
  • Monotonic Rows: Keep your numbers organized in descending order along rows or columns to trigger smooth chain reaction merges.
  • Keep Grid Uncluttered: Plan merges that clear multiple spaces at once to prevent the 16-cell board from filling up prematurely.
  • Victory & Beyond: Creating the 2048 tile triggers a win celebration, but you can keep playing indefinitely to pursue 4096, 8192, and record high scores!
