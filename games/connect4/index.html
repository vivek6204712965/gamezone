🔴 Connect Four 🟡

Red's Turn (Player 1)

About Connect Four

Connect Four is an arcade web adaptation of the world-famous vertical 4-in-a-row strategy game. Drop vibrant red and yellow tokens into a suspended 7-column by 6-row blue grid. Challenge the tactical AI opponent or go head-to-head with a companion in local pass-and-play multiplayer with zero install required.

How to Play

Select your game mode (vs AI or 2 Player) and alternate turns dropping discs into any of the 7 vertical columns. Discs plummet down the column to occupy the lowest available empty slot. The first player to arrange 4 contiguous discs of their color in an unbroken straight line (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) claims victory. If all 42 slots are filled without 4 in a line, the game ends in a draw.

Game Controls

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • Mouse Click — Click top arrow buttons (▼) or columns directly
  • Mode Buttons — Toggle between vs AI and 2 Player
  • Restart Game — Clear board and reset current match

📱 Mobile & Touch Controls

  • Column Tap — Tap any column arrow or cell to drop disc
  • Mode Select — Switch between 1P vs AI and 2-Player modes
  • Restart Game — Tap to begin next round

Rules & Tactical Board Tips

  • Control the Center Column: The 4th (middle) column participates in the highest number of potential winning combinations across the board. Securing center control yields massive strategic leverage.
  • AI Strategic Heuristic: The single-player AI analyzes all 7 columns to: 1) Complete immediate 4-in-a-row wins, 2) Block opponent 3-in-a-row traps, and 3) Prioritize central column development.
  • Avoid Setup Traps: Be careful not to drop a disc that enables your opponent to drop directly on top of you to complete their 4-in-a-row.
  • Record Persistence: Victorious matches won against the AI are tracked and safely stored in browser local storage.
