About Dino Runner

Dino Runner is a cyberpunk modernization of the legendary offline dinosaur sprint. Sprint across a futuristic digital wasteland as speed accelerates relentlessly. Leap over neon cacti arrays and duck underneath airborne pterodactyl flocks to set an unbreakable distance record measured in meters.

How to Play

Press Start Run or hit Spacebar to start running. As the cyber T-Rex accelerates, obstacles appear at unpredictable intervals. Leap over single or cluster cacti, and duck down to shrink your hitbox beneath low-flying pterodactyls. Any collision with an obstacle brings the run to an immediate halt.

Game Controls

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • Space or ↑ (Up Arrow) — Jump over obstacles
  • ↓ (Down Arrow) or S — Duck under flying pterodactyls
  • Space — Restart run when game ends

📱 Mobile & Touch Controls

  • ⬆ Jump — Tap on-screen jump button (or tap canvas)
  • ⬇ Duck — Hold duck button to crouch
  • Start Run / Run Again — Tap on-screen overlay buttons

Rules & Distance Strategy Tips

  • Pterodactyl Flight Paths: Flying pterodactyls cruise at different altitudes. High flyers can be run under standing up; mid-altitude flyers require ducking (↓); ground skimmers require jumping (Space).
  • Hitbox Reduction: Ducking drops the dinosaur's height from 44px down to 26px, allowing safe passage under hovering hazards.
  • Speed Scaling: Running velocity ramps up smoothly as your distance milestone expands, requiring faster anticipatory timing.
  • Distance in Meters: Every meter covered increments your distance score, and your personal best distance is safely stored in browser storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What controls are used to jump and duck in Dino Runner?

On desktop keyboards, press Spacebar or the ↑ Arrow Key to jump, and hold the ↓ Arrow Key to duck. On mobile touchscreens, tap the canvas or use the on-screen jump and duck buttons.

How do I avoid airborne pterodactyls?

Flying pterodactyls spawn at varying altitudes. Low flyers require jumping over, mid-altitude flyers require holding the duck key to crouch underneath, and high flyers glide harmlessly overhead.

Does game speed increase over time?

Yes, the sprint velocity gradually accelerates as your distance increases, requiring quicker reaction times and tighter jump timing the further you run.

How is distance saved?

Your furthest distance (in meters) is stored automatically in your browser's localStorage under 'dino_runner_best'.
