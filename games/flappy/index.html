About Flappy Cyber

Flappy Cyber is a neon-infused modern cyber arcade take on the beloved flap-and-glide reflex challenge. Pilot a glowing cyber bird through a treacherous corridor of glowing digital pipe gates. With tuned gravitational physics, instant touch response, and synthesized sound effects, Flappy Cyber delivers pure one-tap reflex gameplay on both desktop and mobile.

How to Play

Tap anywhere on screen or hit the Spacebar to trigger an upward flap against continuous gravity. Time each flap carefully to glide smoothly through the narrow opening between the upper and lower pipes. Each pipe pair successfully cleared awards 1 point. Colliding with any pipe, the ceiling, or the ground results in an immediate crash.

Game Controls

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • Space or ↑ (Up Arrow) — Flap upward
  • Mouse Click — Click canvas to flap

📱 Mobile & Touch Controls

  • Screen Tap — Tap anywhere on the canvas to flap
  • Tap To Fly / Try Again — Tap on-screen overlay buttons

Rules & Rhythm Survival Tips

  • Gentle Tap Cadence: Rapid multi-tapping will catapult the cyber bird into the top ceiling. Maintain a steady, rhythmic tapping tempo.
  • Target the Lower Threshold: Aim slightly lower than the center of pipe gaps. The upward impulse from a flap carries you upward into safe passage.
  • Stay Level: Anticipate the next pipe's vertical elevation before emerging from the current gate to adjust your altitude early.
  • Personal Best Score: Your highest flight score is saved to local storage so you can continually challenge your personal record.

Frequently Asked Questions

What controls are available to make the cyber bird flap?

You can press the Spacebar on desktop keyboards, left-click your mouse, or tap anywhere on touchscreens to propel the bird upward against gravity.

How is scoring calculated in Flappy Cyber?

You earn exactly 1 point for every pair of neon pipe obstacles the cyber bird successfully flies through without touching.

What causes a game over in Flappy Cyber?

Colliding with any portion of the upper or lower neon pipes, dropping to the floor boundary, or flying above the ceiling boundary causes an immediate crash.

How is the best score saved?

Your best run is automatically recorded in your browser's localStorage under the key 'flappy_cyber_best', persisting across browser sessions.
