About Minesweeper Classic

Minesweeper Classic is an authentic, responsive browser rendition of the iconic strategic puzzle game. Featuring a classic 9x9 beginner matrix concealing 10 deadly mines, digital segment counters, tactile sound effects, and dedicated mobile Dig & Flag modes, this version guarantees that your very first click is 100% safe.

How to Play

Click any cell on the board to begin. The first cell opened is always safe and triggers a generous empty space opening. Numbers revealed inside uncovered cells represent how many mines are touching that specific square (ranging from 1 to 8). Deduce the exact location of mines and plant warning flags to avoid accidental detonation. Uncover all 71 safe cells to claim victory.

Game Controls

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • Left-Click — Dig / reveal covered square
  • Right-Click — Place or remove warning flag
  • Smiley Button (🙂) — Restart board immediately

📱 Mobile & Touch Controls

  • ⛏️ Dig Mode — Tap to activate dig tool, then tap tiles
  • 🚩 Flag Mode — Tap to activate flag tool, then tap tiles
  • Smiley Face — Tap to reset grid and timer

Rules & Deduction Tips

  • Guaranteed First-Click Safety: Mines are algorithmically placed only after you click your opening cell, ensuring you never detonate on turn one.
  • Flood Fill Cascade: Clicking a cell with zero adjacent mines (an empty tile) automatically reveals all neighboring safe tiles in an expanding wave.
  • Flagging Strategy: Use flags to lock confirmed mine squares. This prevents accidental taps from detonating the board.
  • Speed Stopwatch: A digital elapsed-time stopwatch tracks your solving speed in seconds. Your fastest winning time is recorded to local storage.
