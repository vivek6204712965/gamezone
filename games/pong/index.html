About Pong Retro Arena

Pong Retro Arena brings the grandfather of digital video games into the modern web era with crisp neon rendering, dynamic audio synthesizers, intelligent AI paddle tracking, and simultaneous two-player local competition. Rally the puck back and forth in high-speed table tennis duels right inside your browser.

How to Play

Choose between vs AI (single player) or 2 Player (local multiplayer) mode and tap Start Match. Guard your goal line by deflecting the incoming neon puck with your paddle. Score a point whenever your opponent fails to return the puck past their baseline. The first player to score 7 points wins the match.

Game Controls

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • Player 1 (Left): W (Up) / S (Down) or Mouse Movement
  • Player 2 (Right): ↑ (Up) / ↓ (Down)
  • Mode Selection: Click top buttons to toggle AI vs 2-Player

📱 Mobile & Touch Controls

  • ▲ Up / ▼ Down — Tap left paddle control buttons
  • Mode Buttons — Switch between 1P vs AI and 2-Player modes
  • Start Match / Play Again — Tap on-screen overlay buttons

Rules & Spin Physics Tips

  • English & Slice Angles: Hitting the puck with the edge of your paddle imparting sharper rebound angles, sending cutting slice shots past your opponent.
  • Rally Velocity Ramp: As consecutive volleys increase without scoring, the ball accelerates dynamically, putting reflex speed to the ultimate test.
  • Winning Condition: First to reach 7 points wins the showdown (WIN_SCORE = 7).
  • Record Persistence: Human victories against the computer opponent increment your persistent AI win counter in browser storage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do controls work in 1-Player and 2-Player modes?

In 1-Player mode vs AI, control Player 1 with W/S, vertical mouse movement, or touch dragging. In local 2-Player mode, Player 1 uses W/S while Player 2 controls the right paddle using ↑/↓ Arrow Keys.

What is the winning score target?

The first competitor to score 7 points wins the match. When the ball passes an opponent paddle, a point is awarded and the ball serves toward the trailing player.

How does ball speed and reflection work?

Striking the ball near the edges of your paddle imparts extra angle and speed, enabling aggressive angle shots to outmaneuver the AI or your opponent.

How are AI win statistics tracked?

Victories earned against the computer opponent in 1-Player mode are recorded in your browser's localStorage under 'pong_ai_wins'.
