About Snake Neon

Snake Neon is a high-speed modern arcade reimagining of the legendary retro snake game. Steer your luminescent cyber snake across a dark digital grid, devouring glowing energy orbs to lengthen your tail and rack up points. With synthesized retro sound effects, neon particle sparks, and persistent local record keeping, Snake Neon delivers instant retro arcade action directly in your web browser.

How to Play

Press the Start button or hit Spacebar on desktop to begin. Direct the snake toward the green energy orbs while keeping your eyes on the grid borders. Each orb consumed awards 10 points and slightly accelerates the snake's slithering speed, demanding sharper reflexes as your tail expands.

Game Controls

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D — Steer direction
  • Space — Start or restart game
  • P — Pause / resume gameplay

📱 Mobile & Touch Controls

  • ▲ ▼ ◀ ▶ — Tap on-screen directional D-pad buttons
  • Start Game / Play Again — Tap on-screen modal buttons

Rules & Pro Tips

  • Avoid Wall Collisions: Striking any of the four arena borders immediately triggers game over.
  • Watch Your Tail: As your score climbs, your growing body becomes your biggest hazard. Plan sweeping loops along the outer perimeter.
  • Dynamic Speed Ramping: Speed interval tightens progressively as you score, challenging your reaction time.
  • High Score Persistence: Your personal best score is stored in your browser's local storage so you can return anytime to beat your record.
