About Galaxy Defender

Galaxy Defender is a thrilling browser recreation of the monumental fixed-shooter arcade classic Space Invaders. Commanded from Earth's orbital defense line, your starfighter must intercept and eradicate descending formations of alien combatants before their mothership armada descends to planetfall.

How to Play

Steer your defense cannon along the bottom perimeter and unleash vertical laser volleys into the alien grid. The alien ranks march horizontally, dropping downward each time they touch the screen borders. Take cover behind the 4 defense bunkers to shield against incoming plasma bombs, but beware: bunkers erode under sustained bombardment from both sides!

Game Controls

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • ← / → or A / D — Move cannon left / right
  • Space — Fire laser cannon
  • P — Pause / resume gameplay

📱 Mobile & Touch Controls

  • ◀ Left / Right ▶ — Tap direction arrows to steer
  • 🔥 FIRE — Tap center fire button to unleash lasers
  • Engage Battle / Try Again — Tap on-screen overlay buttons

Rules & Tactical Defense Tips

  • Destructible Bunkers: 4 defensive bunkers absorb both enemy ordnance and your own stray shots. Use peek-and-shoot tactics from beneath their eaves.
  • Marching Speed Acceleration: As alien ranks thin out, the remaining invaders march and drop significantly faster. Prioritize side columns to slow their descent.
  • Wave Progression: Eradicating an entire fleet immediately advances to the next wave with tighter drop intervals and fiercer bomb drop rates.
  • Fleet High Score: Your top combat tally is preserved across sessions via browser local storage.
