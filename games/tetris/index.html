About Tetris Block Master

Tetris Block Master is an authentic, full-featured browser edition of the world's most popular falling-block puzzle game. Featuring the official Super Rotation System (SRS) with wall kicks, ghost piece drop projection, dedicated piece hold queue, and progressive speed scaling, this web version delivers high-precision competitive puzzle action without requiring any downloads.

How to Play

Press Start Game to begin dropping tetrominoes into the 10x20 matrix. Maneuver and rotate pieces as they fall to form solid horizontal lines without empty gaps. Completed lines vanish, awarding points and shifting all blocks above downward. Prevent blocks from stacking to the top ceiling to stay alive.

Game Controls

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • ← / → or A / D — Move left / right
  • ↑ or W — Rotate clockwise (SRS)
  • ↓ or S — Soft drop (faster fall)
  • Space — Hard drop (instant drop & lock)
  • C or Shift — Hold current piece
  • P — Pause / resume game

📱 Mobile & Touch Controls

  • ◀ / ▶ — Slide piece sideways
  • ↻ — Rotate tetromino
  • ▼ — Soft drop
  • ⚡ Drop — Instant hard drop
  • Hold — Swap with hold queue

Rules & Scoring Mechanics

  • Line Clear Rewards: 1 line = 100 × level, 2 lines = 300 × level, 3 lines = 500 × level, and a 4-line Tetris = 800 × level points.
  • Level Scaling: Every 10 lines cleared increments your level, accelerating piece gravity.
  • Strategic Hold: Press C or tap Hold to bank an awkward piece for later. You can swap once per falling piece.
  • Ghost Piece Guide: Track the semi-transparent projection on the floor to position your hard drops with pinpoint precision.
