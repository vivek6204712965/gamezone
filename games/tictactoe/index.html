About XO Master

XO Master is an electrifying neon rendition of the timeless pencil-and-paper strategy duel Tic-Tac-Toe. Featuring glowing cyan Xs and radiant pink Os, intelligent tactical computer AI, local pass-and-play multiplayer, and persistent session scoring, XO Master provides pure tactical competition instantly in any browser.

How to Play

Select either vs AI or 2 Player mode. Players alternate turns placing their symbol into an open space on the 3x3 grid (Player X plays first, followed by Player O). The first player to align 3 consecutive marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins the match. If all 9 squares fill without 3-in-a-row, the game concludes in a tie.

Game Controls

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • Mouse Click — Click any vacant grid cell to claim
  • Mode Buttons — Toggle between vs AI and 2 Player
  • Restart Board — Reset grid for a new match

📱 Mobile & Touch Controls

  • Cell Tap — Tap any unoccupied square to play mark
  • Mode Select — Switch between 1P vs AI and 2-Player modes
  • Restart Board — Tap to begin next round

Rules & Tactical Decision Logic

  • AI Tactical Heuristic: The single-player AI utilizes a 4-step tactical evaluation engine: 1) Executes an instant winning line if available, 2) Blocks any immediate opponent win threat, 3) Claims the high-value center square, and 4) Chooses random open positions.
  • Corner Dominance: Starting with a corner cell offers the greatest variety of branching fork opportunities to trap opponents into two simultaneous win paths.
  • Win Tracking: Human victories won against the AI opponent are automatically recorded and saved to browser local storage.
