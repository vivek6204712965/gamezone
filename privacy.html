LEGAL & TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Immediately

Summary: GameZone is a free web arcade hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not require signups, do not collect personal information, and do not track you across the web. Your game scores stay on your device.

1. Overview

Welcome to GameZone (accessible at https://vivek6204712965.github.io/gamezone/). We are dedicated to providing a fast, enjoyable, and private browser gaming experience. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding data collection, storage, and third-party services.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your right to privacy. When you visit and play on GameZone:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to register an account, sign in, or provide an email address or password.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, phone numbers, physical addresses, or financial data.
  • No Ad Tracking or Fingerprinting: We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers, marketing cookies, or user fingerprinting scripts.

3. Browser Local Storage (localStorage)

GameZone uses your web browser's built-in localStorage API solely to enhance your local gaming experience. This data remains entirely on your personal device and is never uploaded or synchronized to remote servers:

  • High Scores & Game Progress: Games like Snake, 2048, and Flappy Bird save your personal best score locally so you can compete against yourself.
  • Favorited Games: When you favorite a game card, your preference is saved locally under the key gamezone_favorites.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser cache and site data for this domain.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

GameZone relies on the following trusted third-party platforms to deliver the website:

  • GitHub Pages: GameZone is hosted statically on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical server logs (including visitor IP addresses and browser headers) for security monitoring, DDoS mitigation, and service integrity in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Supabase Database & Gameplay Telemetry: We use Supabase (PostgreSQL) for game catalog retrieval and lightweight, anonymous gameplay activity metrics. When you play a game, anonymous telemetry events (game_start, game_over, game_complete) recording the game played, play duration in seconds, and final score are logged to understand game popularity and improve the arcade experience. No player identities, names, email addresses, authentication credentials, IP tracking, or personal profiles are ever collected or stored with these records. Database access is strictly secured via Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): We utilize Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID: G-9G3LW44DVS) to monitor aggregated website traffic and gameplay events (such as game sessions started and finished). Google Analytics processes telemetry in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy without collecting personal user profiles.
  • jsDelivr CDN: The Supabase client library is loaded via jsDelivr (jsDelivr Privacy Policy) to ensure reliable script delivery.

5. Advertising & Future Monetization Policy

GameZone is currently 100% free, community-oriented, and ad-free. We do not currently serve third-party advertisements, do not partner with active advertising networks, and do not place advertising or behavioral profiling cookies on your device.

If non-intrusive advertisements or sponsorship partners are introduced in the future to sustainably offset hosting and infrastructure costs, this policy will be updated prior to activation to clearly disclose any partner technologies, cookie policies, and user choice mechanisms.

6. External Links

Our website includes links to external resources, such as our open-source GitHub repository and developer profile. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies when visiting external sites.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect enhancements or adjustments to our website architecture. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have questions, feedback, or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Page or file an issue on our GitHub repository.
